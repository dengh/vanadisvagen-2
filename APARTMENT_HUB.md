## Simple Rules

- Put every new thought in [Inbox](00_Home/Inbox.md) first if you are not sure where it belongs.
- Every task should have an owner, next action, and timing.
- Every decision should include the reason, not just the outcome.
- Keep project pages short. Move old notes into `05_Archive` when they stop being useful.
