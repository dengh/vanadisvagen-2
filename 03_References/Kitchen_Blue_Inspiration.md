## What Works In This Direction

- Deep blue cabinetry feels calm but still distinctive.
- Warm wood beams and stools keep the kitchen from feeling cold.
- Brass knobs add warmth without being loud.
- Stone countertop gives contrast and works with the apartment's character.
- The island remains the social center.

## Things To Validate

- Exact blue shade in real daylight and evening light.
- Whether existing fronts can be painted or should be replaced.
- Whether the stone countertop should stay, be replaced, or be echoed elsewhere.
- How the blue works with the fireplace, beams, floor, and dining furniture.
- Whether black appliances and dark cabinetry make the back wall too heavy.
